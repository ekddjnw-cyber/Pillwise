WHAT YOU MAY NOTICE

Possible side effects

Side effects vary from person to person and depend on the pill. Many are mild, and some improve after the first few months.

Commonly reported effects

↗

Spotting or changes in bleeding

Light bleeding between periods, changes in period timing, or lighter periods can occur, especially when starting a pill or missing doses.

≈

Nausea

Some people feel nauseated, particularly at first. If it persists or is severe, ask a healthcare professional for advice.

✳

Headaches or breast tenderness

Headaches and breast tenderness can happen. New, severe, or unusual symptoms should be assessed rather than automatically blamed on the pill.

◷

Mood or libido changes

Some people report mood changes or changes in sexual desire; others notice no change. Seek support if changes concern you or affect daily life.

GET URGENT MEDICAL HELP

Know the warning signs

Serious complications are uncommon, but certain symptoms need urgent assessment, particularly for people using a combined pill containing estrogen.

  • Sudden chest pain, shortness of breath, or coughing up blood.
  • Sudden weakness or numbness, difficulty speaking, or sudden vision changes.
  • A severe or unusual headache, or severe pain or swelling in one leg.

If symptoms are sudden or severe, contact local emergency services. Do not wait for an online answer.

i

Do not stop or change a prescribed medicine based only on this page. Contact a healthcare professional or pharmacist for advice tailored to you. If you think you may be experiencing an emergency, seek urgent care.
