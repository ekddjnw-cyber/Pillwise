A BALANCED VIEW

Benefits & risks

Understanding both the advantages and the limitations can help you have a more informed conversation with a healthcare professional.

+

Potential benefits

  • Effective pregnancy prevention when used correctly and consistently.
  • Combined pills may make periods lighter, more regular, or less painful.
  • Some combined pills can help with acne or symptoms related to certain menstrual conditions.
  • Some people experience fewer symptoms related to their menstrual cycle.
!

Risks & limitations

  • Possible side effects, including changes in bleeding, nausea, or headaches.
  • Combined pills slightly increase the risk of blood clots; the absolute risk is low for most people but can be higher with certain health factors.
  • Some medical conditions or medicines can make a pill unsuitable or affect how well it works.
  • Pills do not protect against sexually transmitted infections.

Before starting or changing pills

Tell a healthcare professional about your medical history, migraines, smoking or nicotine use, blood pressure, any history of blood clots, and all medicines or supplements you take. This helps them assess which options are appropriate.

If you miss pills, vomit soon after taking a dose, have severe diarrhea, or start another medicine, check the product instructions or ask a pharmacist because the advice depends on the pill and situation.

Remember

Experiences differ. Do not use another person's experience as a substitute for medical advice. A clinician or pharmacist can help you weigh the benefits and risks for your own circumstances.
