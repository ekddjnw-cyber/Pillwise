What are contraceptive pills?

Contraceptive pills are medicines taken by mouth to help prevent pregnancy. Different pills contain different hormones, and the right option depends on a person's health, needs, and preferences.

i

Good to know: Pills do not protect against sexually transmitted infections (STIs). Condoms can reduce the risk of many STIs and can also be used alongside another contraceptive method.
